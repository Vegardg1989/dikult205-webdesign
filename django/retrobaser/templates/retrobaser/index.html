Welcome to Retrobaser!

We here at Retrobaser give you the option to add your favorite games from the retro era. It is community-driven, meaning the input of our users is important to make the site grow. After signing up you can go to the discussion section and suggest games you want to have on Retrobaser. The admins will then have a look, and if it is a suitable retro game, we will add it to the website. We have already added several games. You can rate them and give them a comment: why do you like it, why don't you? Once again, welcome to the newly established Retrobaser!
