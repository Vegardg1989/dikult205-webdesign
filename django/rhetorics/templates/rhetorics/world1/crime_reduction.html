Crime reduction: the work is not finished.

In 2016, shootings in Chicago spiked so sharply that the city lost nearly two decades of progress in a single year. In 2017 and 2018, shootings fell again in most of the city's police districts.

We believe the drop is no accident. Working with the police on precision policing, so resources go to the few places and people behind most shootings, and giving young people real job opportunities both show results in the data.

Policing with precision.

When officers can trust the information they get, they use their resources better. That is why false reports, like the father's story of a gunman in the Chicago Tribune case, cost more than they seem to.
