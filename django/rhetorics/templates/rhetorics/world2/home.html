Welcome to the CRA

We are here to support and protect the rights and freedoms, which the amendments to the United States Constitution grant its citizens. Our goal is to preserve and protect the hunter's right to possess and use firearms for sporting purposes, and to educate the general public about rifle use.

We strongly promote conservation and safe sport hunting as a wildlife management tool. We desire to see the highest degree of sportsmanship and good fellowship and to prevent the occurrence of unsporting behavior. We promote and assist in the planning, construction, and preservation of civilian shooting ranges.

Gun violence is a serious public health problem, where thousands are murdered and harmed each year, including children, which is why our organization is dedicated to educating citizens about the lawful use and possession of firearms.

We arrange safety courses for gun owners in the hope of averting such unfortunate incidents in the future.

A well regulated Militia, being necessary to the security of a free State, the right of the people to keep and bear Arms, shall not be infringed.
