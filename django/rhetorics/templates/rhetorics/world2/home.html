Welcome to the CRA

We are here to support and protect the rights and freedoms that the amendments to the United States Constitution grant its citizens. Our goal is to preserve and protect the hunter's right to own and use firearms for sport, and to educate the public about rifle use.

We promote conservation and safe sport hunting as a wildlife management tool. We want to see the highest degree of sportsmanship and good fellowship. We help plan, build and preserve civilian shooting ranges.

Gun violence is a serious public health problem. Thousands are killed and harmed each year, children among them. That is why our association is dedicated to teaching citizens the lawful use and ownership of firearms.

We arrange safety courses for gun owners in the hope of preventing such tragedies in the future.
